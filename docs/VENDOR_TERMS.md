# Coin Vendor participation and identity processing

Draft for review only. This document is not published as a legal page and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before use.

## Peer-to-peer trading

Coin Vendors participate in peer-to-peer coin trades with other platform users. A vendor is responsible for checking payment in the agreed receiving account before confirming receipt in the trade. Coin escrow is released only after the seller confirms receipt or an authorized marketplace supervisor resolves a dispute. A vendor must not claim payment that was not received, misrepresent payment details, or pressure another participant to confirm early.

## Account and market requirements

Vendors must keep their account information and payment instructions accurate, protect their login credentials, and follow the platform's marketplace rules. The platform may set country availability, trade limits, pricing bands, security bonds and fees. A vendor's displayed quote is locked when a trade begins; later rate changes apply only to new trades.

The platform may pause or suspend new vendor trades while leaving existing open trades available for resolution. Disputes, penalties, security checks and review decisions are recorded for marketplace safety. Vendors must cooperate with reasonable information requests and provide truthful application details.

## Identity data

An applicant consents to provide legal name, residential address, verified phone number, identity document images and a live camera recording for manual review. Identity files are stored privately and encrypted at rest. Access is limited to admins with the vendor identity review permission and each access or decision is audited. Rejected application files are scheduled for deletion after the configured retention period. Revoked vendor files are scheduled for deletion after the configured legal hold period. Minimal review and audit records may remain after identity files are deleted.

Applicants may request deletion of data attached to a rejected application from the application page. A request does not remove records that must remain for an active dispute, legal hold or other required retention.

## Review and liveness

The live camera check asks the applicant to say their name and a one-time code during an 8–15 second recording. A reviewer manually compares the recording with the identity document. Browser recording cannot technically rule out virtual cameras, replay devices or other capture manipulation; approval therefore requires human review.

## Questions and disputes

Applicants and vendors can contact platform support for questions. A dispute should be opened through the trade page as soon as possible. A deadline never automatically releases escrow to either party.
